About Us


"Vijaya Vilas" is an Indian Vegetarian Restaurant famous for serving high-quality food with excellent taste. It is one of the largest chains of indian vegetarian restaurants, brings its own delectable cuisine and artful variations of popular and traditional Indian dishes.

We take pride in serving pure vegetarian food and to experience the true flavors of South Indian home-cooked foods.

As a much-loved Indian caterer in London for the last 9 years and one of the best Indian veg restaurants in Hounslow & Langley , we welcome you with a warm heart and a Menu full of tasty, original flavours of the subcontinent!

Our Team

Chef Koushik Shankar

Koushik Shankar 'The Mad Chef' hails from Chennai. He has worked in some of the most famous kitchens in the world under the tutelage of the greatest stalwarts in the restaurant business.

Chef Peter Joseph

He is using traditional Indian cooking methods for his dishes. He is offering diners the very best quality and a wide range of palate-pleasing flavours whilst remaining true to his heritage.

Chef Sanjeev Kapoor

Sanjeev Kapoor is the most famous and commended chef in India. He is also an entrepreneur. The Indian Government honored him with the Padma Shree Award in 2017.
